Japan in Numbers — Nippon Statistics Atlas. An independent atlas built from published official statistics. Figures come from the Statistics Bureau of Japan, the Cabinet Office, the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare, the Ministry of Finance, MEXT, the World Bank and the OECD, and each panel names its source and year. The site runs entirely from its own files — no network, no tracking.

Source colophon — Statistics Bureau of Japan: Population Census 2020 · Labour Force Survey · Consumer Price Index · Population Estimates · Cabinet Office: National Accounts (2020 benchmark) · Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare: Vital Statistics · life tables · Comprehensive Survey of Living Conditions · Ministry of Finance: Trade Statistics of Japan · MEXT: School Basic Survey · World Bank: World Development Indicators · OECD: Health Statistics.
