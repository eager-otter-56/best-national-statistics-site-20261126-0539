Japan in Numbers — Nippon Statistics Atlas. An independent atlas built from published official statistics. Figures come from the Statistics Bureau of Japan, the Cabinet Office, the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare, the Ministry of Finance, MEXT, the World Bank and the OECD, and each panel names its source and year. The site runs entirely from its own files — no network, no tracking.
